around 3 "lenses": Partnership, People, Place (kept as sections below). CCR-specific updates from

## Small Business Incentives Program — 3 tracks

1. **Rent Subsidy** — reduces overhead in a business's early years. 78 recipients, **69 still operating**.
2. **Façade Improvement** — forgivable loans for distressed-building curb appeal. Of 35 vacant buildings that got one, 33 attracted new tenants (**94% success rate**).
3. **Historic Electric Sign Program** — since 2022, **5 historic signs restored** (e.g. the 1960s Wiedemann Neon sign at Herb & Thelma's Tavern, 718 W. Pike St).

**Participating Covington businesses beat national success-rate averages** (2024 BLS benchmark):

| | Covington | U.S. |
|---|---|---|
| Small business, year-1 survival | **91%** | 80% |
| Small business, 5-yr survival | **88%** | 50% |
| Restaurant, year-1 survival | **91%** | 83% |
| Restaurant, 5-yr survival | **91%** | 50% |

## People

- **"Double Epicenter" branding** — Covington markets itself as "big-city access without the big-city ego," inside a 15-county economy, minutes from 3 pro sports teams across the river. Self-styled "Spicy Mayberry."
- **SparkHaus** — region's newest entrepreneurial hub, grand opening 2025. Backed alongside Aviatra Accelerators and the SBDC Covington office. Attendees at the opening: Christine Russell (NKY Port Authority), Chad Summe (Managing Partner, eGateway Capital), Dave Knox (Blue North), Sen. Chris McDaniel, Judge-Executive Kris Knochelmann, Mayor Ron Washington.
- **Haile Foundation returns to Covington** — 608 Madison Ave, **6 jobs, $900,000 investment**. Ralph V. Haile Jr. (former Peoples Liberty CEO) was instrumental in shaping Mainstrasse Village. President/CEO: Bernie McKay.
- **LifeSciKY** — 15,000 sqft wetlab for biotech startups at OneNKY Center. Exec Director: Christin Godale, PhD. 3 tenant companies:
  - **Simbryo Technologies** — moved HQ Houston→Covington; endometrial fertility platform; $144,000 investment, 5 jobs.
  - **PAN Biologics** — Knoxville-based, cancer-therapy R&D; $500,000 investment.
  - **Clareo Biosciences** — precision immunotherapeutics.
- **Commonwealth Center for Biomedical Excellence** (future) — will house NKU Chase Law + UK College of Medicine's NKY campus; ~600 students/faculty. (Same anchor project detailed in `ACADEMY_RIVERFRONT_CCR.md`'s Block G.)
- **First Kentucky city to adopt the CROWN Act** (bans race-based hair discrimination).
- **Perfect score on the Human Rights Campaign Municipal Equality Index (MEI)** every year since 2022.
- **Inclusive-growth workforce programs:**
  - $25,000 workforce grant → Esperanza Center bilingual workshops, reached 5,000+ residents.
  - 39 Latino high schoolers (Holmes + Holy Cross) in an "Introduction to the Trades" workshop at Enzweiler Building Institute.
  - 73 adults through career-pivot trade/business-startup sessions, several enrolled in advanced Enzweiler training.
  - "How to Establish a Business" workshop + Spanish-language *Guía de Negocios* (business guide), responding to 44% national growth in Latino-owned businesses.
- **Covington Academy of Heritage Trades (CAHT)** — masonry/wood-restoration training. Featured story: Kelsey, who went from CAHT training → leadership role at the Cincinnati Observatory → her own restoration business.
- **Covington Works Program** — 50 businesses engaged, 60 residents got career coaching/training, 8 paid internships.
- **$200,000 + 5-year commitment to Enzweiler Building Institute** — 25% of trade-school spots reserved for Covington residents/students.

## Place

- **Wenzel Distillery** — Covington's first distillery in 100+ years (since Prohibition). 412 Madison Ave, a revived 1920s building. Ribbon-cutting attendees: Judge-Executive Kris Knochelmann, co-founder Austin Dunbar, Mayor Ron Washington, **Gov. Andy Beshear**, co-founders Tony and Amy Milburn.
- **Frank Duveneck House and Studio** — **$600,000 in preservation grants** secured. Duveneck: major American artist, late 19th/early 20th century. 1232 Greenup Street. Historic Preservation Specialist: Kaitlin Bryan.
- **New interactive public art map** — 144 public artworks identified citywide (including Latonia). Supported by ArtsWave and meetNKY; built largely by intern Adebukola Aderanti (graduating University of Cincinnati, Master's in Community Planning).
- **New sculpture, "CAIRN"** by artist MATCOM — 19 pillars, one per Covington neighborhood, coming to Third & Russell. Competition sponsored by the Haile Foundation with Carnegie Visual and Performing Arts Center, the Center for Great Neighborhoods, and ArtsWave.
- **2025 Authenti-CITY Award winners:**
  - Dirt Artisans (Chad Irey) — trails across Devou Park's 700 acres
  - Mainstrasse Manneken Pis
  - KungFood Amerasia (521 Madison Ave; Chef Rich Chu & son Johnny; featured on Guy Fieri's *Diners, Drive-Ins and Dives*)
  - Emily Wolff & Paul Weckman — 5 Covington culinary venues + public art
  - Bard Burgers & Chili (3620 Decoursey Ave, Latonia — home of "the Bardzilla")
- **Covington Time Capsule** — placed May 2025 in the Covington Central Riverfront neighborhood, 100+ items from residents/businesses, **sealed until May 2075**. Partners: Kenton County Public Library (Latonia branch), LINK NKY, NK Monuments.
- Other 2025 recognitions: HGTV "40 Most Charming Small-Town Downtowns in America"; National Arbor Day Foundation "Tree City of the World."
