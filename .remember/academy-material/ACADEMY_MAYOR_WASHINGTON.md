# Mayor Ron Washington

Source: `academy-material/Mayor's Academy Materials.pdf` (press releases + articles, 2024-2025).

## Quick facts

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Sworn in** | January 3, 2025, at The Carnegie (public ceremony) |
| **First African-American** elected mayor | In any of Kentucky's five largest cities |
| **Oath administered by** | KY Supreme Court Justice **Michelle Keller** (Kenton County resident) |
| **Bible used** | Supplied by Covington Professional Firefighters Local 38 |
| **Spouse** | Angela Washington, at his side for the oath |
| **Background** | Grew up in Covington; career in law enforcement; first ran for mayor in 2020 |
| **Predecessor** | Mayor **Joe Meyer** — Washington credits him with progress and with encouraging him to run |
| **Prior role** | City Commissioner, then Mayor Pro Tem (through 2024) before winning the mayor's race |

Video: "Covington Inauguration" (filmed by TBNK) — his remarks begin at the 16:42 mark.

## Stated priorities (from his own words, Jan 2025 press release)

- **More housing for all income levels**
- **A joint recreation commission with schools** for youth activities/programs (see below — he created this within months)
- Philosophy: "a door and a mentality open to stronger collaboration at all levels"
- Goal: spread Covington's momentum to the "furthest corners of the city" — not everyone feels the benefit of the recent jobs/energy yet

His own youth memories of Covington (used in later press materials): baseball at Athey Ave & Craig St, free throws at Goebel Park, summer lunch at 3rd District School.

**Named partners/allies:** KY federal delegation, Gov. Andy Beshear, Sen. Chris McDaniel, Rep. Stephanie Dietz, Kenton County Judge-Executive Kris Knochelmann.

## Covington Recreation Committee (created via mayoral order, June 2025)

- Created at a Board of Commissioners meeting; announced June 12, 2025.
- **15 members total.** **3 co-chairs**: Vice Mayor **Shannon Smith**, Commissioner **Tim Acri**, and Board of Education member **Kareem Simpson**.
- Members chosen **jointly** by the Board of Education and Board of Commissioners.
- City + Covington Independent Public Schools (CIPS) staff play a non-voting support role.
- **Tasks:** comprehensive inventory of youth recreational opportunities; align programming and share facilities between city and schools; identify service gaps; engage youth/community stakeholders.
- CIPS Board Chair quoted in coverage: **Tom Haggard** (grew up playing soccer at Latonia Elementary; later a Covington Summer Rec Program youth worker; coached Holmes boys varsity soccer).

## Black Business Roundtable (2024, while Mayor Pro Tem)

- Led by then-**Mayor Pro Tem Ron Washington**.
- Convened with partner organizations to address the unique needs of Black-owned businesses.
- Partners in that space: Greater Cincinnati African American Chamber of Commerce, Aviatra Accelerators, Esperanza Center of Northern Kentucky, Pride Center of Northern Kentucky.
