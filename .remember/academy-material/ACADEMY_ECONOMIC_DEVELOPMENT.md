# Covington Economic Development

Source: `academy-material/Mayor's Academy Materials.pdf` — the 2025-2030 strategy press release (June 2025) and the City's 5th annual Economic Development Impact Report (2024 data). Department: **City of Covington Economic Development**, led by Director **Tom West, AICP**.

## The 2025-2030 strategy: "Action to Impact: Building on Momentum"

- Adopted by the Board of Commissioners, June 2025. Authored by **Camoin Associates** (Richmond, VA).
- Builds on the prior 2019 strategy, **"From Opportunity to Action"** by Garner Economics (Atlanta).
- Informed by 60+ stakeholders (focus groups, steering committee, interviews).
- **Tom West's framing:** turning vacant buildings into office space — the opposite of the national post-pandemic trend. "Jobs follow people, people don't follow jobs like they used to."

### Six strategic goals

1. **Advance the Covington Central Riverfront (CCR)** as a vibrant economic asset — dedicated Development Manager, tenant attraction strategy, cultivate a developer mix. See [[ACADEMY_RIVERFRONT_CCR]].
2. **Most supportive place to start/grow/relocate a business** — streamline business navigation, track/promote real estate, serve diverse business owners, use business intelligence.
3. **Elevate Covington's reputation** as a destination for business & talent — talent attraction, amplify support via events, assert regional identity/value.
4. **Grow target industries**: Professional Services, Digital and Creative, Life Sciences and Technology, Experiencing Covington (hospitality/tourism), Made in The Cov (manufacturing).
5. **Nationally recognized entrepreneurial hub** — coordinate with SparkHaus, LifeSciKY, Aviatra.
6. **"Beyond business"** — education/talent/workforce dev, housing, placemaking/mobility (Econ Dev plays a supporting role here, not the lead).

### Recognition

- **Money Magazine's Top 50 Best Places to Live (2024)** — cited reasons: CCR redevelopment, inclusivity, small business support, Cincinnati skyline views. Narrative arc used in city materials: "most blighted city in the nation" (1983) → top-50 list (2024).
- CCR: **Gold Award, Innovation in Economic Development**, International Economic Development Council (IEDC).
- thecovky.gov: Silver Winner, Economic Development Website, IEDC (2024 and 2023).
- **Municipal Equality Index: perfect score of 100, third year running.**
- **First city in Kentucky to adopt the CROWN Act** (bans race-based hair discrimination).

### Sector spotlights (2024)

- **Life & Bioscience:** PPD Clinical Research (Thermo Fisher Scientific) — 65,000 sqft expansion in Latonia, **252 new jobs**, avg wage **$72,000**, **$42M** capital investment. Also GoGo Meds (digital pharmacy, +100 jobs) and a $15M Commonwealth-funded Covington Life Science Partners wet lab opening 2025.
- **All Things Office:** RoveIQ (+7 jobs, $150K investment) built the "Madison Mile" interactive wayfinding kiosks (City + meetNKY + Southbank Partners + RoveIQ).
- **Made in The Cov:** Maverick Chocolate Co. (bean-to-bar) opening production + retail. Covington's first industrial spec building in decades fully leased within 12 months — 364 new jobs, 3 businesses, 146,800 sqft.
- **Entrepreneurship:** Aviatra Accelerators expanded into a 4,578 sqft "Aviatra Incubator" at 112 Pike St — helped **1,029 women entrepreneurs** region-wide (+16% since 2023). KY Small Business Development Center (Innovation Alley): 114 clients coached, 146 jobs supported. **Sparkhaus** — 40,000 sqft entrepreneurial hub opening summer 2025 (Blue North + NKY Port Authority + Kenton County).
- Visitor entertainment up **212%** (Placer.ai data); 30 ribbon cuttings for new/expanded retail & restaurants.

### Workforce/talent programs

- **Covington Academy of Heritage Trades (CAHT):** 24 students, 18 scholarships ($41,000 total), $24K in preservation grants. Won 5 national/state/local awards in 2024.
- **Covington Works Program** (w/ NKADD, 3rd year): 10 entrepreneurs matched with 16-week paid NKU internships; 49 Build ED scholarships; 12 people got transportation assistance to/from work.
- **Enzweiler Building Institute's Covington Campus** (opened 2022, Latonia) — trades training for high schoolers. City funding **$200,000 over a 5-year commitment**; 25% of spots reserved for Covington residents/students.
- **Esperanza Latino Center** — City grant for 5 bilingual workforce-dev/entrepreneurship initiatives, targeting the Latino community and Holmes High School students.
