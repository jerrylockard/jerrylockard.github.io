# Covington civic meeting schedule (reference)

Standing facts about Covington's recurring public meetings, last confirmed 2026-08-23.
No automated monitoring exists for this anymore — check
[onboard.covingtonky.gov](https://onboard.covingtonky.gov) and the city's own event
pages before relying on this if it's been a while.

## Standing recurring civic events

| Event | Cadence | Time | Location | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Board of Commissioners** | Weekly, Tuesdays | 6:00 PM ET / 5:00 PM CT | City Hall, 20 W. Pike St, Covington, KY 41011 | Alternates caucus and legislative meetings. |
| **Kenton County Planning Commission** | Monthly, first Thursday | 6:15 PM ET / 5:15 PM CT | Kenton County Government Center, 1840 Simon Kenton Way, Kenton Chambers (2nd Floor) | |
