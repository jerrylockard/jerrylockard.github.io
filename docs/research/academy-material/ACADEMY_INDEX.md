## Quick orientation if you only read one thing

Covington in one paragraph, per this material: a historically blighted river city (per the city's own framing) now on Money Magazine's 2024 Top-50-Places-to-Live list, mid-transition from City Manager to Mayor-Council government (effective 2027), led since January 2025 by its first Black elected mayor, leaning hard into economic development (the 23-acre Covington Central Riverfront, a possible law/med school anchor) while facing a real regional housing shortage and a real federal-jobs budget hit.
