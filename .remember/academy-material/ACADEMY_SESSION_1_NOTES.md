## Government structure (as explained in-session)

- **Current form:** City Manager form — the City Manager (Reddy) handles administrative
  operations.
- **Transitioning to:** Mayor-Council form — the Mayor oversees departments directly.
- **Legislative body today:** Board of Commissioners — handles budgets and policy.
- **Legislative body after transition (2026):** City Council, 6 members.

This is the same transition covered in more legal/procedural depth in
