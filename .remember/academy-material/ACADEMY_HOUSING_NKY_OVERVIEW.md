# Northern Kentucky Housing Crisis — Overview & Strategy Menu

Source: `academy-material/Mayor's Academy Materials.pdf` — **"Home for All: Northern Kentucky Housing Strategies"** report, published January 2025. Coordinated by **NKADD** (Northern Kentucky Area Development District) + **Brighton Center / Brighton Properties**. Funded by Interact for Health + United Way of Greater Cincinnati. Site: **HousingNKY.org**.

This is the single largest topic in the Academy material — see [[ACADEMY_HOUSING_TOOLS_CASE_STUDIES]] for the 4 deep-dive tools and real-world case studies this overview points to.

## The core numbers

| Fact | Value |
|---|---|
| **New homes needed over next 5 years** (2023 NKY Housing Data Study) | **6,650 units** |
| Median home value change since 2016 | **+70%** (2023: $216,200) |
| Median rent change since 2016 | **+47%** |
| Single-family homes as % of regional housing stock | 67% |
| "Missing middle" (2-9 unit buildings) as % of stock | Only **12%** |
| Region covered | **8 counties**: Boone, Campbell, Carroll, Gallatin, Grant, Kenton, Owen, Pendleton |

### Three target personas the whole menu is designed around

| Persona | Wage | Monthly budget | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Essential workers (nurses, teachers, first responders) | 1-2 earners @ $45K/yr each | $1,150/mo | 2+ bedroom home |
| Young adults, first household | ~$11/hr | Up to $500/mo | Studio or roommate apartment |
| Downsizing seniors | Fixed/Social Security income | $550-1,850/mo | 1-2 bedroom home |

Early childhood educators are called out specifically: **$15-18/hr** puts them squarely in the workforce-housing target band.

**Why employers care:** Site Selection Magazine ranks income-aligned housing as a **top-10 location factor** companies weigh when deciding where to locate — "homes are where jobs sleep at night."

## Process behind the report

- 90+ community leaders/experts through 2024. 40+ community meetings + a digital survey since the Sept 2023 data study.
- Engagement stats: **12 Data Walks, 34 public presentations, 692 residents reached.**
- Top 5 resident priorities when choosing a home: (1) income-aligned monthly payment, (2) close to work, (3) like the neighborhood, (4) number of bedrooms, (5) close to family/friends.
- Presented to the KY General Assembly Housing Taskforce, KY Housing Corp Conference, and a KY Chamber listening session.
- Dedicated to **Pat Crowley (1961-2024)**, a committee chair/steering member who died before publication.

## Glossary (from the report's Appendix)

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **AMI** | Area Median Income — HUD-calculated income eligibility metric |
| **AHTF** | Affordable Housing Trust Fund |
| **Cost burden** | Spending >30% of income on housing costs |
| **Cost burden, severe** | Spending >50% of income on housing costs |
| **CDBG** | Community Development Block Grant (HUD, via KY Dept for Local Government) |
| **FSS** | Family Self-Sufficiency Program — HUD program to build savings/earnings for rental-assistance recipients |
| **HOME** | HOME Investment Partnerships Program — federal block grant for affordable housing |
| **IRB** | Industrial Revenue Bond |
| **LIHTC** | Low Income Housing Tax Credit (federal) |
| **LMI** | Low to Moderate Income — a tract where 50%+ of households earn <60% AMI, or poverty rate ≥25% |
| **Missing middle housing** | Duplex/triplex/fourplex/townhome-scale housing between single-family and large multifamily |
| **QAP** | Qualified Allocation Plan — criteria for awarding housing tax credits |
| **Section 8 / HCV** | Housing Choice Vouchers — low-income households choose/lease private rentals |
| **TIF** | Tax Increment Financing |
