# Covington Central Riverfront (CCR)

Source: `academy-material/Mayor's Academy Materials.pdf` — City of Covington article (Mar 2024) + 2024 Economic Development Impact Report.

## What it is

**23-acre former IRS site**, just west of Madison Ave, north of Fourth St. The single largest active redevelopment project in Covington.

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| Facility decommissioned | 2019 |
| City purchased site | 2020 (during COVID) |
| Demolished by | O'Rourke Wrecking, 2022 |
| Master plan | **Cooper Carry** (Atlanta) |
| Engineering/infrastructure | **KZF Design** |

## The law school / med school anchor

KY Senate 2024-26 budget proposed **$150M** state allocation (later reported as **$125M** in the 2024 Impact Report) to relocate:
- **NKU's Salmon P. Chase College of Law** (currently Highland Heights)
- **University of Kentucky's School of Medicine — Northern Kentucky campus** (opened on NKU's campus 2019, graduated its first class May 2023; a 4-year school, faculty livestreamed from UK Lexington, taught partly by St. Elizabeth Healthcare physicians)

Both would relocate to **Block G** of the CCR site — bringing **~600 grad students/faculty/staff**. Would sit within 2 miles of Greater Cincinnati's 25 largest law firms and 5 federal/state courthouses. Nearby existing life-sciences cluster: CTI Clinical Trial and Consulting Services, Gravity Diagnostics, Bexion Pharmaceuticals.

## Site layout

- **Block G** — law/med school (above)
- **Block B** — 16 for-sale townhomes on Fourth St (Drees Homes — see 2024 development agreement below)
- **Block O** — 2.5 acres, the largest single parcel, commercial user TBD as of the 2024 article
- **Blocks A/M/N** — early-stage negotiations as of 2024

## Build phases

1. **Phase 1** — fill dirt (27,000 + 30,000 cubic yards) to floodwall height; sewer/gas/electric/water/comms lines; street grid restoration between Third and Fourth St; Russell St promenade. Public cost ≈ **$15M**.
2. **Phase 2** — parking garage below development; public park with levee-top river views; sidewalks.
3. **Phase 3** — north side of the levee wall, connecting to the multi-mile **Riverfront Commons** trail.

## 2024 milestones and development agreements (from the Impact Report)

- Reconnected street grid substantially complete.
- **Gold Award Winner, Innovation in Economic Development** — International Economic Development Council (IEDC).
- "Topping Out" ceremony marked the final scoop of fill dirt on the new Third Street — drone show + fireworks by JS Held/Bray Construction/KZF Design.
- Streetcraft promotional video passed 500K views, 800+ comments.

| Developer | Project | Value |
|---|---|---|
| **Drees Homes** | 16 for-sale townhomes (Block B) | **$7M** |
| **Silverman and Company, Inc.** | 257 apartments + 7,700 sqft retail + 194 parking spaces | **$67.2M** |
| **Funke Real Estate Group** | 10 single-family homes + 2 corner mixed-use buildings | **$8.45M** |
| State funding | Phase 1 public infrastructure | **$10M** |
| Federal RAISE grant | Phase 2 public infrastructure | **$16.3M** |
| State funding | Chase Law School + UK Med School relocation | **$125M** |

Quote on record: **Ryan Silverman**, VP, Silverman and Company Inc. — "The CCR is an incredibly exciting opportunity, and the City has created a robust, yet thoughtful, live/work/play vision for the site."

## 2025 update (from the 2025 Economic Development Impact Report magazine)

Numbers moved since the 2024 table above — treat these as the current figures:

| Developer | 2024 figure | **2025 figure** |
|---|---|---|
| Silverman & Company | $67.2M / 257 apartments | **$83M / 270+ apartments**, 7,700 sqft commercial |
| Funke Real Estate Group | $8.45M | **$8.5M** (rounding, not a real change) |
| Drees Homes | $7M | $7M (unchanged) — broke ground on the 16 townhomes in 2025 |

- **Funke broke ground on Block A in 2025**: 10 single-family homes + 2 smaller commercial endcap buildings.
- **Phase 2 public infrastructure RFP issued** — 600-space parking garage + land bridge — **awarded to Messer Construction**.
- **New RFPs issued for Block C and Block F** — both envisioned as mixed residential/retail, the next phase after the above.
- Full current site map (blocks A/C/D/E/F/G/H/I/J/K/O + future hotel + future convention center expansion) exists in the magazine — not transcribed here since it's visual, not factual data; see the source PDF if the layout itself matters.
