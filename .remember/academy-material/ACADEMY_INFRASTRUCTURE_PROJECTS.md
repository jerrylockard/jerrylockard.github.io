# Big Infrastructure Projects: City Hall + Brent Spence Companion Bridge

Source: `academy-material/Mayor's Academy Materials.pdf` — LINK nky (Oct 2024) and a June 2025 joint KY/OH press release.

## New Covington City Hall

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| Groundbreaking | Week of October 29, 2024 |
| **Site** | 600 block of Scott Street — former Emergency Shelter of NKY site + small park |
| Neighbors | Kenton County Library branch (north), post office (south) |
| Renderings by | Brandstetter Carroll Inc. and Elevar Design Group (Oct 2023) |
| Built by | Pepper Construction |
| **Target opening** | **Summer 2026** |

**Why it matters / backstory:** then-Mayor Joe Meyer noted city government has occupied **5 different locations in 54 years**. A citizen task force convened in 2018 to define the building's purpose — they wanted it visible, accessible, centrally located, and a genuine civic commons with community-event space, "not just a fortress."

**Attendees at the groundbreaking:** Kenton County Judge-Executive Kris Knochelmann, Commissioner Ron Washington, Commissioner Tim Downing, Commissioner Shannon Smith, Mayor Joe Meyer, Commissioner Steve Hayden, Independence Mayor Chris Reinersman, Dayton Mayor Ben Baker.

## Brent Spence Companion Bridge

Part of the **8-mile Brent Spence Bridge Corridor Project**. Design unveiled **June 18, 2025** by Gov. Andy Beshear (KY) and Gov. Mike DeWine (OH).

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| Design | **Cable-stayed independent-deck bridge** (cheaper/easier to build than a steel truss) |
| Similar to | Abraham Lincoln Bridge (Louisville), Veterans Glass City Skyway (Toledo) |
| Managed by | **KYTC** (Secretary Jim Gray) + **ODOT** (Director Pamela Boratyn) |
| Existing Brent Spence Bridge (built 1963) | Reconfigured to 3 lanes/deck + emergency shoulders — carries **local** Covington↔Cincinnati traffic |
| New companion bridge | Carries **I-71/I-75 through-traffic**. **No tolls.** |
| Status (June 2025) | FHWA concurred with bridge-type selection; moving to final engineering/construction |

Bonus detail: northbound drivers on the new bridge's top level get a new, unobstructed view of the Cincinnati skyline. Info: www.BrentSpenceBridgeCorridor.com.
